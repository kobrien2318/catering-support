Frequently asked questions

Questions before getting started?

Here are some of the questions businesses commonly have about working with ClearPoint.

Is ClearPoint only for large catering companies?

No. ClearPoint was designed with catering and other high-volume service businesses in mind, but the operation can also support smaller businesses. If your team occasionally struggles to keep up with customer calls, additional coverage may be useful.

Are you replacing our existing customer-service team?

No. The service is designed primarily to supplement your existing team. You remain in control of when calls are forwarded and how ClearPoint fits into your operation.

When can ClearPoint answer our calls?

Coverage can be structured around your needs, including peak periods, evenings, weekends, events, seasonal demand, or other times when your team needs additional support.

How do you know what to say to our customers?

During onboarding, we establish your preferred call-handling procedures, scripts, information requirements, and escalation instructions. ClearPoint follows those guidelines when handling your calls.

Can ClearPoint represent our brand and communication style?

Yes. Calls can be handled according to your approved brand voice, scripts, terminology, and customer-service procedures. The goal is for ClearPoint to function as an extension of your team rather than as a disconnected third party.

What if we only need occasional help?

Occasional support is one of the service models available. Businesses can use coverage for individual needs, short periods, events, or other situations where additional capacity is helpful.

Do all service arrangements require a long-term contract?

No. Shared and occasional support can be structured flexibly. Dedicated account support is a separate service model with a contractual commitment because it involves account-specific onboarding and dedicated recurring coverage.

How much does the service cost?

Pricing depends on the type of coverage, expected volume, schedule, and level of support required. We offer flexible service models and can discuss the appropriate option during a consultation.
